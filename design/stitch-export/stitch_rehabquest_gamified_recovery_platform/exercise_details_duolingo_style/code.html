Safety Note

Don't hold your breath! Keep breathing naturally while engaging your muscles.

How to do it

1

Position yourself

Sit or lie on a flat surface with your legs extended straight in front of you.

2

Contract your quad

Tighten the muscle on the top of your thigh by pushing the back of your knee down into the surface.

3

Hold and Release

Hold the contraction for 5 seconds, then slowly relax and repeat for the next rep.
