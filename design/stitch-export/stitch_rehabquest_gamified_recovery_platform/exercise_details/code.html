Safety Note

Keep your back flat and move slowly.

How to Perform

1

Sit or lie on your back with your legs straight out in front of you.

2

Tighten the muscle on the top of your thigh by pushing the back of your knee down into the floor.

3

Hold for 5 seconds, then relax for 5 seconds. Repeat for the prescribed number of reps.
